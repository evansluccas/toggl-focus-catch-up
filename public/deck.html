Prioritisation

What I chose not to build

  • Ask for rates in onboarding — fails if they don't have them yet
  • Fix the first-session bugs — can't show an absence in 60s
  • Strip the team framing — ambient, not a moment
  • Reconstruct the week already worked

Tradeoffs

Left on the table

  • Invoicing — the obvious next step
  • Profitability trends — can't exist in week one
  • Team surfaces — wrong persona
  • AI guessing your day — you tell us, we don't guess

Impact

What I'd measure

  • Non-zero Amount in week one — near-zero today by construction
  • D7 return, split by Catch Up completion
  • Trial→paid for switchers — they already have revenue
  • Guardrail: imported entries later edited or deleted

How I used AI

Where I overruled it

  • Worked — audited the product, extracted their design system, built fast
  • Overrode — its onboarding-fields recommendation
  • Overrode — “that direction is overdone.” The evidence didn't hold

It states a weak inference as confidently as a verified fact. Knowing which is which is the job.
